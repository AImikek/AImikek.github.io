Site privacy

Updated October 22, 2026

The sites and apps measures visits, link clicks, device category, referring website, scroll depth, and active time to help improve the page. It uses a random browser identifier that expires after 30 days; it does not identify you by name or email.

Full referring-page addresses and raw IP addresses are not saved in the analytics database. An hourly hashed network identifier helps limit automated submissions and expires after two hours. Visit records expire after 90 days; deletion is handled asynchronously by Google Cloud. Analytics is processed using Google Firebase and Cloud Functions and is visible only to the site owner.

Enable Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in your browser to opt out of sites and apps analytics. The page respects either signal. Browser storage may be cleared at any time to remove its random identifier.

Other linked apps and services have their own sign-ins and privacy practices. The central admin hub does not share household credentials with the public website.
