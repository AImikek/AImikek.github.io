01The opportunity and commercial objective +

Rudolph Pickleball currently needs approximately 80 players for a tournament to perform well. At roughly $40 per player, that represents approximately $3,200 in registration revenue, with Eric estimating $800–$1,200 in profit depending on format and direct costs.

The objective is to increase the percentage of tournaments that reach profitable capacity, reduce the time Eric spends manually pursuing registrations, grow repeat attendance, develop sponsor revenue, and create a scalable playbook for additional events and locations.

Based on Eric’s current figures, one additional profitable tournament in a month may represent approximately $800–$1,200 in additional operating profit. This is an illustrative scenario, not a guarantee.
02Initial 30-day growth sprint +

Fee

$1,000 paid upfront.

Primary build-out

  • Implement a professional email-marketing platform and migrate the existing list.
  • Clean, organize, and segment contacts where the available data allows.
  • Create reusable tournament announcement, reminder, and last-chance email templates.
  • Deploy and measure initial email campaigns.
  • Plan, build, and launch a branded Rudolph Pickleball website and centralized link page.
  • Research, register, and connect an appropriate Rudolph Pickleball domain, with the registration paid directly by Eric.
  • Organize links for registration, tournament schedules, WhatsApp, social accounts, sponsors, and future locations.
  • Design a compliant text-message opt-in flow and select an appropriate texting system.
  • Create a repeatable tournament marketing calendar and campaign checklist.
  • Review the registration funnel and recommend improvements to messaging, pricing urgency, retention, and referrals.
  • Create an initial sponsorship strategy and inventory of available sponsor placements.
  • Deliver a concise end-of-month performance review and next-stage roadmap.
The first month involves the most setup. Once the systems and templates exist, future campaigns should become substantially faster and more efficient.
03Ongoing partnership and scope +

Monthly base

$1,000 per month, paid upfront.

The base covers an agreed, limited scope of fractional CMO strategy and marketing-technology execution.

Strategy & planning Monthly promotion calendar, offers, messaging, retention, referrals, sponsorship strategy, and growth priorities.
Email & audience Campaign creation and deployment, list growth, basic segmentation, reporting, and reusable reminders.
Website & systems Website and link-page management, domain support, email systems, and SMS opt-in development.
Growth optimization Registration messaging, retention ideas, offer strategy, and recommendations based on campaign performance.

Reasonable operating cadence

  • Up to four primary email campaigns per month.
  • Reminder or last-chance sends using established templates when appropriate.
  • One monthly strategy and performance call.
  • Ongoing optimization based on engagement and registration data.

Outside the monthly scope

  • Daily social-media management or responding to DMs.
  • Customer service, player support, or tournament-day operations.
  • Filming, photography, or substantial video editing.
  • Bracket management, Swish administration, or scorekeeping.
  • Graphic design, full custom software development, or major website expansions beyond the agreed initial build.
  • Paid-ad management, major sponsorship fulfillment, or work for Eric’s unrelated businesses unless separately agreed.
  • On-demand availability seven days a week.

Substantial work outside the agreed scope will be discussed and priced separately before it begins.

0430% participation in new business growth +

In addition to the monthly base, Mike will receive 30% of incremental operating profit generated by any new Rudolph Pickleball revenue initiative launched while the partnership is active.

This applies whether the new opportunity is conceived, sourced, or initiated by Eric, Mike, or both together. The purpose is to recognize that the systems, marketing, and ongoing support will free Eric’s time to develop additional business while helping new initiatives launch and grow successfully.

Examples of qualifying new business

  • Additional tournaments beyond the established operating schedule.
  • New facilities or geographic locations, including Jensen, Wellington, or Miami.
  • New leagues, recurring formats, clinics, memberships, or paid player programs.
  • Corporate, charity, private, or branded events.
  • New partnerships, digital products, or other Rudolph Pickleball revenue streams.

How incremental operating profit is calculated

Revenue from the new initiative, minus only the direct costs required to deliver it, such as facility fees, direct event staffing, prizes, medals, processing fees, refunds, direct advertising, and other costs approved by both parties in advance.

General overhead, unrelated business expenses, Eric’s personal time, and expenses from other businesses will not reduce the calculation. Performance compensation will be calculated and paid monthly with reasonable access to the relevant revenue and direct-cost records.

05Sponsorship revenue and player experience +

Sponsorships can create meaningful cash revenue while also improving the tournament experience through products, hydration, food, prizes, wellness activations, giveaways, content, and sponsor promotion.

Mike may source, pitch, and bring mutually approved sponsors into Rudolph Pickleball tournaments and marketing.

Cash sponsorships originated and closed by Mike

Net cash sponsorship revenue will be split 50% to Rudolph Pickleball and 50% to Mike. “Net” means cash collected from the sponsor minus only direct, pre-approved fulfillment costs specific to that sponsor.

Non-cash partnerships

Product, giveaway, food, beverage, wellness, prize, or experiential sponsors may be accepted by mutual approval when they improve the player experience, even when no cash is paid. Non-cash value does not create a cash commission unless separately agreed.

Existing relationships

Sponsorships sourced directly through Eric’s existing relationships remain Eric’s unless Mike is asked to manage, expand, negotiate, or close the opportunity and both parties agree on compensation beforehand.

06Software, domain, and third-party expenses +

The monthly fee covers Mike’s strategy, implementation, and agreed work. All third-party business expenses must be paid directly by Eric or Rudolph Pickleball using a company credit card or Eric’s personal credit card.

Examples

  • Email and SMS software.
  • Domain registration, renewals, hosting, and website services.
  • Automation, analytics, and social scheduling tools.
  • Paid advertising, premium design assets, stock media, or other approved services.
Mike will not be expected to front recurring subscriptions or routine business expenses, manage reimbursements for small receipts, or finance company operating costs. Eric will enter his payment card directly into each approved service whenever possible.

Any exceptional expense that must be paid by Mike requires Eric’s written approval in advance and prompt reimbursement.

07Ownership, access, and continuity +

Rudolph Pickleball will retain access to its domain, website, link page, contact databases, marketing accounts, campaign data, final branded templates, and completed Rudolph-specific deliverables after all amounts due are paid.

Accounts should be created in Rudolph Pickleball’s name or transferred to Eric when practical, with Mike receiving the access needed to perform the work.

Mike retains ownership of his pre-existing intellectual property, PB Tourney, generic tools, reusable code, frameworks, processes, know-how, and materials not created exclusively for Rudolph Pickleball.

08PB Tourney and other partnership benefits +

Complimentary tournament entries

Up to two complimentary team entries per month, subject to availability. Each entry covers Mike and one player designated by Mike.

PB Tourney promotion

Mike may feature PB Tourney in mutually approved Rudolph Pickleball marketing as a tool for casual play, game logging, round robins, recreational tournaments, and related player experiences.

PB Tourney will not initially be positioned as a replacement for Swish because Swish currently provides broader tournament-management functionality used by Rudolph Pickleball.

If PB Tourney later develops functionality that is comparable or superior to Swish, Mike and Eric may mutually agree to update its positioning and explore broader use within Rudolph Pickleball.
