Frequently asked questions

My Instagram was hacked and the email was changed. Can it still be recovered?

Often, yes. Instagram has a dedicated hacked-account process that can work even when the attacker changed your email and phone number. The right path depends on what the attacker changed — that's exactly what the review covers.

My account was disabled by Instagram. Is that recoverable?

Disabled accounts have an appeal process, and many are restored — especially when the disable was triggered in error. Timing and the way the appeal is written matter.

Will you need my password or a login code?

Never. Recovery is done through Instagram's own official processes on your side. Anyone asking for your password or verification codes is a scam.

How fast will I hear back?

You'll get an automatic confirmation right away, and a personal follow-up with next steps shortly after.

Is this an official Instagram or Meta service?

No — this page is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta. It's personal guidance on navigating their official recovery processes correctly.
