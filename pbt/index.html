✨ App Guide
Everything PB Tourney can do — free, works on any phone, installs to your home screen. Updated with the ✨ assistant, Community and level group chats, group messages, notifications, friends, fair-fight matchups, final standings and reliability scores.
🧭
Getting Around
Bottom bar: 💬 Chat (groups, messages, friends, assistant), 📅 Games (upcoming sessions to join), 🎮 Live (tonight's tournament games), 📊 Stats (standings, podium, photos) and 👤 Profile. The pink ➕ New Game button up top does the three things you came for: schedule a sign-up game, start a tournament right now, or ask the assistant. ❤️ is your notifications, 🏆 the leaderboard, ☰ everything else.
✨
The Assistant
Chat → ✨ Assistant. Tell it what you want in plain English: "set up a game at Brickell Place Thursday at 7", "invite everyone 4.5+ to my next game", "post tomorrow's game to the 4.0+ Crew", "who are the top 5 doubles players?". It asks only when something's missing, then does it and shows you the result. It can create sessions, invite people (by level, group, name or your friends), post to groups with the game attached, message players, pull stats, open screens, and also cancel a game (recalling the invites and telling people), take back invites, or delete a tournament when you clearly ask. It cannot merge players or edit scores.
📲
Install It Like an App
Open mikek.ai/pbt in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen (Android: menu → Install app). You get our icon and the app opens full screen with no browser bars. The 📲 Install the App item in the menu walks you through it. When a new build ships, a blue New version available pill appears — tap it once.
❤️
Notifications
The ❤️ next to the trophy collects everything addressed to you: new direct messages, activity in your level groups, new followers and game invites. The pink number is your unread count. Tap an item to jump straight there. (In-app for now — no lock-screen pushes yet.)
⚖️
Fair Fights & Smart Schedules
When a round robin starts, the app loads everyone's 💎 DPR and picks the seating that keeps both teams' combined DPR as close as possible in every game. Rotations are pre-solved designs: nobody partners twice, byes are even and never back-to-back, opponents spread evenly. With 9+ players the organizer picks how many games each (7, 8, everyone-partners-once…). Rosters show the lineup's average DPR and every round lists who's sitting.
🎯
DPR Reliability
Next to every rating is a reliability %, like DUPR's. It climbs with games played (about 95% by 25 games) and fades after months without playing, so a 4.9 from two games last spring is flagged as low reliability. Put your DUPR on your profile: it becomes your starting point, so your first games move you from your real level instead of from 4.25. Tap 📤 Share on your rating to send your ranking and a link to your profile. The rating itself never decays — only how much we trust it. Singles and doubles are rated separately; your profile has a toggle for each.
🏆
Leaderboard Minimums
To rank in doubles you need 10 games all-time, 5 in the last 30 days, or 3 in the last 7 days. Players under the bar still appear in "Almost There" below the table with how many games they need. Singles shows everyone until the pool grows. Every past tournament also carries a 💎 level (average DPR of the field) so you can see which nights were the strongest.
🚀
Tournaments in 30 Seconds
Pick a format, type player names, hit Start. The full schedule generates instantly — courts, sides, and matchups included. Round Robin rotates partners so everyone plays with everyone. 4-Player Rotation runs best-of-3 matchups with rotating partners — and it flexes to any player count. Best part: zero DUPR pressure — play loose, try new partners, and compete hard without anything touching your official rating.
📝
Log Casual Games
No schedule? No problem. Log Games mode lets you add games one at a time during open play. The session names itself after the players and date, and every score still counts toward your all-time stats.
📲
Everyone Edits Scores Live
Invite friends with the Share button, link, or QR code right from the Games screen. Anyone who joins sees every score update in real time on their own phone — and can enter scores too. No more shouting the score across four courts.
🏟
Find Courts Near You
The Find Courts page (menu) maps every pickleball court in the Miami area — hours, pricing, lights, indoor/outdoor, and one-tap directions. Allow location and the whole list sorts by distance from you. Know a hidden gem? Submit it right from the page.
📖
New to Pickleball? Quick Start
The Quick Start Guide (menu) takes you from zero to your first game: the two videos worth watching, the best Instagram coaches to follow, where to play, and the local WhatsApp community. Share your own tips at the bottom — the best ones make it into the guide.
📅
Games to Join — Reserve Your Spot
Planning a session? Create it in Games to Join (menu): set the location, date, time, spots, and level — then share ONE link. Friends tap it, sign in, and claim numbered spots. Full sessions get an automatic waitlist that promotes people when someone drops. Make it Public so the whole community can find it, or Private for just your crew. Weekly games can repeat with one tap, and when everyone shows up — start the tournament straight from the roster. 🥒
🗓
One-Tap Calendar
Every scheduled game has Google Calendar and Apple Calendar buttons — tap once and the event lands with the right time, place, and details. No more "wait, what time Sunday?"
💎
DPR — Our In-House DUPR
Every player gets a DPR (Diamond Performance Rating) on the familiar DUPR scale, computed from every game ever logged here. It works like the pros' rating: every point counts against expectation — who you played WITH, who you played AGAINST, and the score. Hang close as the underdog and your rating climbs even in a loss. Under 10 games shows PROV (provisional) until your number settles. Updates the moment scores go in.
💬
Chat, Messages & Friends
Open Chat from the menu. A 🌎 Community group for everyone on the app, plus level groups 5.0+ Elite, 4.5+ Club, 4.0+ Crew and 3.5+ Open — each open to that level and up. Groups you haven't unlocked yet show small and grey with how far away you are. Your level is your 💎 DPR once you have 5 games, otherwise the DUPR on your profile. Messages are private chats: search a name or tap 💬 Message on any profile, or tap 👥 Group to start a group thread like iMessage (name it, tick people, add more later). Group member counts only count people with accounts; "+N not on app" are players who've been in tournaments but never signed up — share the app link with them. Organizers also get 📱 Group Text Everyone Signed Up on a session page, which opens Messages with everyone's number. Follow players from their profile and they land in your Friends tab with one-tap messaging. Each group shows who's in it (everyone at that DPR and up) — tap 👥 members. The ❤️ Notifications button next to the trophy collects everything: new messages, group activity, new followers and game invites. From any session page you can 🎟 Invite Friends (they get a notification) or 💬 Post to Chat. In any chat, the 🎟 button attaches an upcoming game to your message as a card with a View & Join button — no links to paste. Everyone follows Mike by default and Mike follows you back; unfollow any time.
🏅
How Winners Are Decided
The round robin seeds everyone. Smart rule: if everyone played the same number of games, rank by wins (Avg Diff breaks ties); if counts differ (byes, late arrivals), rank by Avg Diff (points scored minus allowed, per game) with Win % breaking ties, because raw wins can't be compared across unequal games. The app re-checks the counts after every score, so adding a late arrival, an injury, a cancelled game or an extra game can flip the table between wins and Avg Diff. Anyone who played fewer than half the games is flagged ⚠ and not seeded into finals; anyone with no games sits at the bottom. The Stats page says which rule is in effect and why. The top 8 play finals: 1&4 vs 2&3 for the championship and 5&8 vs 6&7 for 5th–8th. The finals decide the podium: championship winners are champions, their opponents finish 3rd, and the 5th–8th game decides 5th through 8th. Everyone else follows by round-robin rank. Finals are kept out of the round-robin table so they can never leapfrog a higher finalist. Tap ℹ️ How rankings work on the Stats page any time.
📊
Your Stats Tell Your Story
Tap any player — including yourself — for the full story: 💎 rating with your rating journey graph, club ranking, 🔥 best win streak, 🤝 best partner (who you actually win with), 😈 top rival with the head-to-head record, 🧊 clutch record in 2-point games, and your games-per-month. Receipts are forever — settle the argument with data.
🔁
Run It Back
When a tournament ends, organizers hit Run It Back and next week's session posts in seconds — same place, same time, same rules, date moved a week, with last week's lineup ready to ride along. That's why the next game is already up before you've left the court. Grab your spot early!
📸
Group Photos
After you play, hit + Add Photo in the Photos section on the Stats page. Anyone can add them, and they save to that tournament forever — a year from now the archive will be gold.
👑
The Leaderboard
Tap the 🏆 Leaderboard for the rankings: your name stays frozen on the left while you swipe sideways through 💎 DPR, W-L, Win %, Avg Diff, games, and points. Tap any column header to re-rank — including 💎 to sort the whole club by rating. Switch Doubles/Singles and 7-day, 30-day, or all-time windows (quiet weeks hide themselves). Tap any player for their full profile. Play 3+ games to get ranked.
🏆
Champions & Stats
Finish a session and the Stats page crowns your champion with a celebration. Standings auto-calculate wins, points for/against, and average differential. Share a public results link anyone can view without signing in, or save the standings as an image for the group chat.
📜
Past Games
Every finished session is archived automatically in Past Games. Reopen any past tournament to relive the standings, sort the stats, and share the results — your whole pickleball history in one place.
👤
Your Player Profile
Head to My Profile and add your name, city, Instagram, official DUPR, home court, and a photo. Your 💎 DPR card sits right at the top, computed live from your games. One login connects everything — your sign-ups, your scores, your stats, your spot on the leaderboard.
💬
Shape What Gets Built
This app is built WITH its players. Tap Give Feedback in the menu — feature requests go straight to the builder, and the best ideas ship. Brackets, city battles, and more are on the roadmap.
🔐
Simple, Secure Sign-In
Sign in with your phone number or Google — no passwords to remember, ever. Your data is yours: only you can edit your profile and history.
