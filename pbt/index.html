✨ App Guide
Everything PB Tourney can do — free, no downloads, works on any phone.
🚀
Tournaments in 30 Seconds
Pick a format, type player names, hit Start. The full schedule generates instantly — courts, sides, and matchups included. Round Robin rotates partners so everyone plays with everyone. 4-Player Rotation runs best-of-3 matchups with rotating partners — and it flexes to any player count. Best part: zero DUPR pressure — play loose, try new partners, and compete hard without anything touching your official rating.
📝
Log Casual Games
No schedule? No problem. Log Games mode lets you add games one at a time during open play. The session names itself after the players and date, and every score still counts toward your all-time stats.
📲
Everyone Edits Scores Live
Invite friends with the Share button, link, or QR code right from the Games screen. Anyone who joins sees every score update in real time on their own phone — and can enter scores too. No more shouting the score across four courts.
🏟
Find Courts Near You
The Find Courts page (menu) maps every pickleball court in the Miami area — hours, pricing, lights, indoor/outdoor, and one-tap directions. Allow location and the whole list sorts by distance from you. Know a hidden gem? Submit it right from the page.
📖
New to Pickleball? Quick Start
The Quick Start Guide (menu) takes you from zero to your first game: the two videos worth watching, the best Instagram coaches to follow, where to play, and the local WhatsApp community. Share your own tips at the bottom — the best ones make it into the guide.
📅
Games to Join — Reserve Your Spot
Planning a session? Create it in Games to Join (menu): set the location, date, time, spots, and level — then share ONE link. Friends tap it, sign in, and claim numbered spots. Full sessions get an automatic waitlist that promotes people when someone drops. Make it Public so the whole community can find it, or Private for just your crew. Weekly games can repeat with one tap, and when everyone shows up — start the tournament straight from the roster. 🥒
👑
The Leaderboard
Tap the 🏆 Leaderboard anytime to see the all-time rankings — who's the real champ in your city. Sort by Average Point Differential (the fair way), Wins, or Points. Win streaks get 🔥 badges, the #1 spot gets the crown, and every row shows how far you are behind the player above you. Tap any player to see their full match history. Play 3+ games to get ranked.
🏆
Champions & Stats
Finish a session and the Stats page crowns your champion with a celebration. Standings auto-calculate wins, points for/against, and average differential. Share a public results link anyone can view without signing in, or save the standings as an image for the group chat.
📜
Past Tournaments
Every finished session is archived automatically in Past Tournaments. Reopen any past tournament to relive the standings, sort the stats, and share the results — your whole pickleball history in one place.
👤
Your Player Profile
Head to My Profile and add your name, city, Instagram, DUPR rating, and home court. Your profile connects your games to your leaderboard identity — and it auto-saves as you type.
💬
Shape What Gets Built
This app is built WITH its players. Tap Give Feedback in the menu — feature requests go straight to the builder, and the best ideas ship. Brackets, city battles, and more are on the roadmap.
🔐
Simple, Secure Sign-In
Sign in with your phone number or Google — no passwords to remember, ever. Your data is yours: only you can edit your profile and history.
