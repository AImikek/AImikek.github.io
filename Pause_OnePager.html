02 — The Mechanism

An AI revenue layer on top of your booking system. Three levers, zero new headcount.

Lever One

One-to-One Reactivation

AI reads each client's actual history — services booked, visit rhythm, package expiry, what they tried once and never repeated — and writes them an individual message. The client who did three floats and stopped hears about float. The one who books shots but never IVs hears about the needle-free option. Thousands of one-person campaigns, booked directly into open slots.

Lever Two

24/7 Lead Response

Every inquiry, DM, and missed call answered in seconds, qualified, and booked — across all locations. The paid traffic you already buy converts instead of going cold.

Lever Three

Capacity Yield

Perishable off-peak slots — sauna, cryo, float — filled with targeted offers to the right segment at the right moment. Pure-margin revenue from hours you're already staffed for.
